Build Your System
→ Talk to Xanadu (button)
When a user clicks “Talk to Xanadu,” they don’t land on a traditional contact page, they enter a guided system designed to quickly understand their business and route them to the right support inside Xanadu. The flow begins with a short introduction screen that sets expectations and explains that the goal is to identify their needs and connect them with the correct division. Then the user goes through a simple qualification process starting with what they need help with (such as growth, technology, investment, market expansion, or idea development), followed by their current stage (startup, scaling company, or established business), and finally a short open question about their biggest challenge. After answering, the system analyzes their responses and routes them to the most relevant part of Xanadu, whether that is Consulting, Soft, Ventures, Sports, Trading, Web3, or Investments,  so the experience feels personalized and direct. Once routed, the user is shown a final decision screen where they can choose between a free short alignment call or a paid strategic deep-dive session with a senior strategist. From there, they book a time and receive confirmation that their case will be reviewed and handled with the appropriate level of expertise, making the entire experience feel structured, intentional, and high-value rather than a generic inquiry form.
